# Citi Bike | Demand and Fleet Allocation

Stevens team case study comparing demand models and bicycle allocation scenarios.

The team used ggplot to examine demand against time of day, neighborhood income, and household vehicle access. It compared linear regression and random forest predictions for **five stations: 402, 426, 435, 497, and 519**, then used demand estimates in constrained allocation scenarios.

The source presentation documents the team analysis. The original data and full solver workbook were not located. The scenarios do not measure operational outcomes.
